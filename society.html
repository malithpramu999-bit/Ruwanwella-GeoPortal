HISTORY
Ruwanwella, meaning "Ruwan Wisirunu Wella" (a place where gems was scattered), is a historic town linked to the Sitawaka Kingdom. Its heritage includes the Dutch Fort, Jubilee Ambalama, and Pethangoda Uyana, reflecting its royal, cultural, and colonial history.
